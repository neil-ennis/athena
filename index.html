FAIR QUESTIONS

Let’s keep
it simple.

We already use ChatGPT. What would Athena add?+

The next step is making it repeatable: your templates, your approved information, clear instructions and a consistent review process. Harrison helps turn occasional prompting into a workflow your team can use again.

Do we need to be technical?+

No. You need to understand your business and be willing to try a new way of doing one task. Setup includes practical training and written instructions for the person who will use it.

What does it cost?+

Start with a free introductory conversation. Harrison is inviting expressions of interest for one founding-client pilot with no implementation fee: one agreed workflow, a small test and a handover. Suitability and scope are agreed first. Software subscriptions are separate, approved by you and held in your own account. Further work is quoted before it starts.

What happens to our business information?+

Before setup, you agree which information the chosen tool can access and where it will be processed. Testing can start with fictional or redacted examples. Access is kept to what the task needs. A local setup can be explored where appropriate; suitability and cost need to be assessed.

Will AI send messages or make decisions for us?+

The initial workflows prepare drafts and flag missing information. Your team reviews the output and approves anything sent to a customer. AI can make mistakes, so checking the result is part of the process.

Will we need new software?+

Possibly, but the starting point is what you already use. Some workflows use ChatGPT or the Codex desktop app with your own subscription. Harrison will explain the requirements, agree any costs and test the workflow on your intended plan before handover.
